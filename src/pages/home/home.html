Veda Sunassee

Founder

Veda Sunassee has spent a decade in several roles with the African Leadership (AL) Group, first as an Entrepreneurial Leadership Faculty at the African Leadership Academy before later serving as the Founding Dean of the African Leadership University (ALU), Rwanda for 2 years.

Veda is a facilitator, emcee and moderator and has worked with the likes of the Allan Gray Orbis Foundation, ELMA Philanthropies and Facebook.

Veda is a graduate of Princeton University and was selected among the inaugural Obama Leaders in 2018.

Veda recently re-joined ALU as its Executive Director.
