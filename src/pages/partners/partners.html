Partner criteria

We are looking for partners.

  • Access to high-potential students with real financial need
  • Ability to verify admissions, tuition and eligibility
  • Willingness to help shape the future of education funding
  • Commitment to simple and transparent progress updates
  • Strong alignment with our mission and values
